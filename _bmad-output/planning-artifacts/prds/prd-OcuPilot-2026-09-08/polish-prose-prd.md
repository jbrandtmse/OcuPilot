Lens: Editorial prose (bmad-review). Reviewed: `prd.md`, 17,313 words (word_metrics.py, 2026-09-08). Reader type: humans. Style guide: Microsoft Writing Style Guide. Purpose: a launch-grade PRD feeding UX, architecture and epics work and a solo builder.

The structure lens ran first (`polish-structure-prd.md`). Its two CUT passages (the §10.1 closing sentence on the 22-large-rows figure; the streaming half of the `[NOTE FOR PM]` paragraph) were skipped. Fixes that land inside a MOVE'd passage (§10.1 opening paragraph, §2 bullet 4, §11) apply at the passage's new home; fixes inside a CONDENSE'd passage are noted so the condensed rewrite absorbs them.

Owner constraints honored: no fact, number, date, row ID, FR/UJ/SM/NFR/Open Question number, class name, path or decision is changed. Glossary terms are checked verbatim; synonym drift is flagged, not renamed into new terms. Code spans, frontmatter, table structure and headings were skipped. Every Original Text is an exact, unique string in the file.

## Purpose and audience read

This document exists to help the UX, architecture and epics authors and the one developer building OcuPilot know what it must do, for whom and in what order, traceable to the catalog, so they can act on it daily through the 2026-09-27 deadline without re-reading the brief, the research or the catalog.

## Style, tone and voice

- **Voice.** Declarative, clinical, present tense; no hedging, no contractions, no first person. Sentences carry their qualifier at the end as a contrast ("not hidden", "not merely gated", "never as a half-working full form"). Enumerations are colon-led and semicolon-chained. Absolutes ("never", "only", "exactly one") are the requirements register, not overstatement. Preserve all of this.
- **Scaffolds.** Fixed formulas do the document's work: "Realizes UJ-n. Catalog: XX-nn.", "Consequences (testable):", "Done when ...", and the six-field journey schema. They are not rewritten here; a few rows fix wording inside them.
- **Intentional choices to keep.** The refrains in §1 and §2 ("Two lines do not move", "Nobody ships an assistant inside the IRIS portal") serve the human reader. No em-dash appears in running prose (verified); the glossary separators are exempt by house style. Numbers are sometimes spelled out ("forty", "eleven", "one thousand"); Microsoft style uses numerals from 10 up, tabled once as a Consider row because the value never changes. The document consistently omits the serial (Oxford) comma; Microsoft style uses it. That is a whole-document choice and is left to the owner rather than tabled 300 times; the rows below add a comma only where its absence changes the reading.
- **Spelling.** US spelling throughout, with four British stragglers (row 15).
- **Register for the audience.** The prose assumes an IRIS-literate reader and uses a few undefined working terms ("rung", "write key", "enforced-state settings"). Two are tabled where a downstream reader would stall; "enforced-state settings" is used consistently and left alone.
