# Structure review: PRD OcuPilot

Lens: Editorial structure (bmad-review). Reviewed: `prd.md`, 17,313 words (word_metrics.py, 2026-09-08). Reader type: humans. Style guide: Microsoft Writing Style Guide. Length target: none. Companion `addendum.md` not reviewed; it is a valid MOVE target.

Owner constraints honored: content is sacrosanct. No functional requirement, consequence, glossary entry, journey, open question or catalog row citation is proposed for removal. Every row is a suggestion the owner accepts or rejects.

## Purpose and audience read

This document exists to help the UX, architecture and epics authors, and the one developer building OcuPilot against the 2026-09-27 contest deadline, know what OcuPilot must do, for whom and in what order, traceable to the 538-row catalog, without re-reading the brief, the research report or the catalog.

Primary readers are humans: three downstream skill authors and a solo builder who will act on it daily for 19 days. The owner reads it as reviewer.

## Summary

- **Recommendations:** 26. MOVE 4, MERGE 1, CUT 2, CONDENSE 9, QUESTION 4, PRESERVE 6.
- **Estimated reduction if all are accepted:** about 750 words, 4.3% of 17,313, leaving about 16,560. About 800 (4.6%) if the §5 index in row 18 is declined. The two largest savings (rows 3 and 4, about 375 words) are moves to the addendum, so no content is lost.
- **Length target:** none was set, so none is assessed. The PRD skill's own rule, that a launch-grade PRD runs as long as its requirements need and downstream detail goes to the addendum, is met by rows 3 and 4; all 79 FRs and their consequences, the glossary, the six journeys, the seventeen open questions and every catalog citation remain in the main document.
- **The finding that matters most is not a cut.** Rows 1 and 2 move 583 existing words forward at zero cost so that a reader meets the commitment, the floor and the success criteria at word 600 instead of word 13,300. Everything else is hygiene.
- **Comprehension trade-offs:**
  - Row 8: §6 stops being a self-contained invariants list for an architecture reader who starts there; each NFR keeps a pointer, but the reader must follow it.
  - Row 3: post-contest planners must follow a link to the addendum or extract-stages.md for stage inventories.
  - Row 2: "Validates FR-n" lines become forward references; acceptable in a pyramid, but a first-time reader will not yet know the FR titles.
  - Row 16: removes a deliberate refrain; keep it if the owner values the echo between §1 and §2 more than the 20 words.
- **Pacing (human readers):** the document has no diagram. A single lifecycle figure for FR-17 (propose, confirm, execute as the user, change event, highlight, audit marker) would anchor the mechanism that §1, §5.3, §7.1 and NFR-6 each describe in prose. That is an addition outside this pass and is noted for the owner, not tabled. NFR-6 (row 25) is the only pacing fix with no word cost.
