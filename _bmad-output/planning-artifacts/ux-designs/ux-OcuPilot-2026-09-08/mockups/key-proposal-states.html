The destructive button is unavailable (Material’s 38%) until the typed name matches exactly — the one place opacity is allowed, because it carries nothing the user must read; a mismatch on blur is reported as “Does not match”, never silently. No Reverse line: a delete has no reversal, and the card only carries one where a reversal exists.

The enforced state shows in three places at once: the restrained banner at the top of the panel, the card’s restrained left bar and status, and the footer’s read-only line — which is always present, so the mode is learnable, and takes restrained while a read-only state applies. Nothing here uses error or destructive: nothing went wrong. The per-user and definition forms of the footer line are “Read-only: on — for you” and “Read-only: on — by the definition”.
