Direction A — Bridge

The logo's navy becomes the chrome: rail and header are one solid navy band that frames a light, dense instrument surface, and teal is the single action color so every button, link and selected item reads as "do something". The navy-to-teal gradient is rationed to hairline indicators (active rail entry, panel header rule) and robot yellow is kept for exactly two meanings: the agent's own identity and the thing that just changed.

Density is the point: 13px type, 36px rows, 6px radii on cards with Material 3 pill buttons and tonal chips, so a 1,000-row list and a long transcript both fit an administrator's screen. Dark mode flips only the content and panel surfaces (see token comment); the navy chrome is the same in both, which is what makes a screenshot unmistakably OcuPilot.
