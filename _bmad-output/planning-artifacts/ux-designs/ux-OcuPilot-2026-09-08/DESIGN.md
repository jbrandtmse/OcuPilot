## Brand & Style

OcuPilot is the ship's bridge for an IRIS instance: a dense, light instrument surface framed by a solid navy chrome, with the agent co-pilot docked on the right the way VS Code docks its secondary side bar. The register is **bold and branded** — a screenshot must be unmistakably OcuPilot — but the brand is carried by the frame, not the content. The logo's navy owns the rail, the header and the status bar; the side bar, the content and the panel sit on Lantern's near-white surfaces so that a 1,000-row table and a long transcript do the talking.

**Foundation.** Angular Material 3 with the Angular CDK. This document is the brand-layer delta over Material's tokens: the color roles, the type ramp, the radii, the density, and the visual spec of OcuPilot's own components. Whatever the sections below do not override inherits Material 3 as-is — state-layer opacities (8% hover, 10% focus, 12% pressed), ripple, scrim, the 38% disabled opacity, checkbox, radio, switch, form-field and tab anatomy. Theme **V3 Lantern**; direction **A, Bridge**; light on first load, with the dark token set defined and contrast-checked here so the polish-week theme toggle is a flag flip.

This document and EXPERIENCE.md take precedence over any mock, wireframe or import.

**When this document and EXPERIENCE.md disagree.** EXPERIENCE.md wins on behavior and copy — what happens, in what order, and the words on screen, the accessible name among them. This document wins on visual treatment — color, type, spacing, radius, elevation, and placement within a region. A fact that is both is split along that seam rather than decided wholesale: EXPERIENCE.md says a notice appears and what it reads, this document says what it looks like and where it sits. Where one document contradicts itself, the other resolves it. Neither document states the other's kind of fact without pointing at it.

Three colors carry three meanings, and they never trade places:

| Color | Meaning | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Navy (`{colors.shell}`) | The instance's chrome | Rail, header, status bar. Constant across light and dark. |
| Teal (`{colors.secondary}`) | *Do something* | Every button, link, selected item, active tab and running indicator. The sole action color. |
| Yellow (`{colors.agent-accent}`, `{colors.agent-container}`, `{colors.change-highlight}`) | *The agent* | Its avatar's ground, its rationale and expected impact, the bar on its proposal card, and the one row or field it just changed. Never a control. |

The logo's navy-to-teal gradient is rationed: the header runs from `{colors.shell}` to `{colors.shell-edge}`, and everything else that carries it is a 2px hairline — the panel header rule and the panel's docked edge. It never fills a surface, and the active rail indicator is a solid `{colors.secondary-dark}` bar, not a gradient (Colors rule 2).

The mark mirrors the product: an eye whose iris is a globe (watching the instance), a winged left edge, and a docked panel on the right holding a yellow robot. The UI quotes it literally. The horizontal reversed lockup ([imports/OcuPilot-Lockup-horizontal-reversed.png](imports/OcuPilot-Lockup-horizontal-reversed.png) — the mark plus the wordmark filled in `{colors.on-shell}` from the artwork's own letterforms) sits directly on the navy header at 32px with no plate; the globe iris is the favicon ([imports/OcuPilot-Favicon-source.png](imports/OcuPilot-Favicon-source.png)); and the robot cropped from the master artwork ([imports/robot-avatar.png](imports/robot-avatar.png)) is the agent's avatar beside its messages and cards. The wordmark is never typeset — Inter is the interface face, not the brand's — and the mark alone ([imports/OcuPilot-Mark-transparent.png](imports/OcuPilot-Mark-transparent.png)) stands in where only the mark fits. The human has no avatar.

### Rules

1. **The chrome does not change with the theme.** `shell` is navy in both modes (it only deepens in dark), so anything drawn on it takes the dark-mode variant regardless of theme: the focus ring is `{colors.focus-ring-dark}` with a `{colors.shell}` halo, the attention dot is `{colors.agent-accent-dark}`, teal on the chrome is `{colors.secondary-dark}`. The light-mode `{colors.focus-ring}` is 1.18:1 against the shell and must never be drawn there.
2. **Gradients are the header and two hairlines, nothing else.** The header runs `{colors.shell}` → `{colors.shell-edge}` left to right. The only other gradients are 2px lines: the panel header rule (horizontal, navy to teal — the artwork's left-to-right reading) and the panel's docked-edge stripe (vertical, teal at the top to navy at the bottom — the docked panel's top-to-bottom reading). Their far stop is the logo's vivid teal `#2090A0`, deliberately kept out of the token set: white on it is 3.78:1, so it never sits under text and never fills an area. The active rail indicator is solid `{colors.secondary-dark}` (6.72:1 light / 8.97:1 dark on the shell): as a gradient its upper half measured 1.94–2.47:1.
3. **Teal is text only where it clears 4.5:1.** On `surface` and the container surfaces it does (5.56:1 and up). On `code-surface` in light mode it is 2.69:1, so links inside a code block or the raw log view are `{colors.on-code-surface}`, underlined, not teal.
4. **Yellow is never a control.** No button, link, switch, chip or badge uses `agent-accent`, `agent-container` or `change-highlight`. The attention dot on the rail's agent entry is the agent's identity, not a control.
5. **Two kinds of disabled.** *Unavailable* (no selection, invalid form) is Material's 38% opacity. *Privilege-gated* (FR-4: shown disabled with the resource named, never hidden) stays readable: `{colors.restrained}` text or `{colors.on-shell}` at 45% on the chrome, no hover state, the tooltip on hover and focus where the control takes DOM focus (rail items, side-bar entries, tiles, buttons). Where focus is virtual — menu items and command-box results — the reason is a trailing `{typography.caption}` inside the item in `{colors.restrained}` (6.55:1 on the menu sheet), never a tooltip. A gated entry the user cannot read is a hidden entry.
6. **Semantic pairs travel together.** A status color is always drawn as `<role>` on `<role>-container` (chips, banners, badges) or as `<role>` text on a surface where the table above says it passes. Never `success` text on `warning-container`, never a container as text.
7. **Dim by role, never by opacity.** A state that recedes — expired, canceled, stopped, gated — is drawn in `{colors.restrained}` (body text in `{colors.on-surface-variant}`) at full opacity. Opacity over the light sheet drags every status color under 4.5:1 (the 60% expired card measured 2.77–4.38:1), and a receded card is still read — a reloaded transcript is made of them. Opacity is reserved for Material's *unavailable* controls, which carry nothing the user must read.

## Typography

One sans for the interface, one monospace for anything the instance would recognize as an identifier: **Inter** for text and **JetBrains Mono** for code, both under the SIL Open Font License (OFL), both shipped as vendored `woff2` in the bundle (NFR-10 forbids any CDN), with `system-ui` and `ui-monospace` as the fallbacks. Neither face is the brand's: the wordmark is the artwork asset and is never typeset (see `logo-lockup`).

| Role | Size / weight / leading | Where |
|---|---|---|
| `display` | 16px / 600 / 1.25, −0.01em | The screen title: the locator bar's current segment; the title of a full-page editor, wizard or viewer. The largest text in the product. |
| `title` | 14px / 600 / 1.3 | Panel header, proposal card title, dialog title, form section title, card title, empty-state title, stepper step labels. |
| `body` | 13px / 400 / 1.4 | Everything read at length: table cells, transcript, form values, menu items, button labels (at 600), the agent's rationale and expected impact on the proposal card. |
| `caption` | 12px / 400 / 1.35 | Chips, banners, the context chip, last-update stamp, the proposal card's countdown, diff labels, unchanged-fields disclosure and guard caption, form labels and helper text, area-tile labels and their screens line, the panel's footer captions. |
| `label` | 11px / 600 / 1.2, +0.04em | Eyebrows: column headers, side-bar area heading, the header's "Namespace" eyebrow, status bar items, severity chips, the "Agent's rationale" / "Expected impact" headings, the Changed tag, the example card's band. Uppercase only for eyebrows (side-bar heading, namespace, the two proposal headings) and column headers; the status bar, tags and chips keep sentence case. |
| `code` | 12px / 400 / 1.5, monospace | Identifiers and payloads: web application paths, class and resource names, namespaces, pids, task names as the instance stores them, diff values, tool-call names, log lines, the raw log view, code blocks, keyboard hints. |

Rules:

- 13px `body` is the floor for running text; `caption` and `label` are for short strings that sit beside something larger. Nothing readable is set below 11px.
- Anything the instance would treat as an identifier is `code`, in tables and in prose alike, so an administrator can tell `%Development` the resource from Development the flag at a glance.
- Numbers in tables are tabular (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) and right-aligned.
- Weight 600 marks the current thing (selected side-bar entry, locator current segment, button labels, the "after" value in a diff); 700 does not exist in the UI — the wordmark's weight lives in the artwork.
- No text is uppercased except `label` eyebrows and column headers. The agent's prose, banners and errors are sentences (see tone in EXPERIENCE.md).
- The wordmark is never typeset. Where the name appears as a brand it is `logo-lockup`'s asset; in running text "OcuPilot" is an ordinary word in the ambient style.

|---|---|---|

**Scale.** A 4px base: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32. Inside a component the gaps are 4 and 8; between controls in a bar, 8; card and panel padding, 12; between regions and around the content column, 16; between form sections and inside dialogs, 24; 32 is reserved for the space above an empty state. Rows are 36px so that `body` text sits in a 36px cell with 12px horizontal padding; log lines are 28px **[ASSUMPTION]**, dense enough for a tail.

**Content column.** The locator bar, the command bar and the table stack with no gap; the table is the content. A full-page editor is a single column at most 720px wide with fields no wider than 480px, and a sticky 56px Save/Cancel bar at the bottom **[ASSUMPTION]** (the assumption is the widths; the sticky bar itself is decided). Large editors put their sections in tabs.

**Home.** The panel widens to `{spacing.panel-home}` over a 120ms width transition (none under reduced motion). That width is `min(50vw, viewport − {spacing.rail-width} − {spacing.content-min-width})`, so the content column always keeps its minimum. The content shows the six `area-tile`s in an auto-fit grid (`{spacing.tile-min-width}` minimum, `{spacing.2}` gaps) under a one-line locator; the tiles **wrap** to a second or third row rather than force the column wider, so Home never scrolls horizontally at a supported width. Beneath the tiles, the instance line (server · version · namespace · flag · user). Polish-week additions (system information, favorites, recents) go above or beside the grid.

**Yield order.** When the row between the header and the status bar does not fit, regions yield in a fixed order, at every width: (1) the side bar collapses; (2) the panel shrinks from its target (the remembered width, or `{spacing.panel-home}` on Home) to `{spacing.panel-min}`; (3) the content column holds `{spacing.content-min-width}` and scrolls horizontally inside itself. Never an overlay, a bottom sheet, a panel close, or a scrolling header, rail or status bar; the page body never scrolls horizontally. The widths that result (rail 48, side bar 240, content minimum 640, panel minimum 320, remembered panel 400):

| Viewport | Side bar | Panel on Home | Content on Home | Panel on a list route | Content on a list route | Yield |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1,920px | open (240) | 960 | 672 | 400 | 1,232 | none |
| 1,440px | Home: collapsed · list: open | 720 | 672 | 400 | 752 | Home: 1 |
| 1,280px | collapsed | 592 | 640 | 400 | 832 | 1 |
| 1,024px | collapsed | 336 | 640 | 336 | 640 | 1, 2 |
| 900px | collapsed | 320 | 532 visible of 640 | 320 | 532 visible of 640 | 1, 2, 3 |

A collapsed side bar is a yield, not a preference: the remembered open state returns when the width allows. The behavior (what the user can do about it, which routes remember what) is EXPERIENCE.md › Responsive & Platform.

**Reflow.** Every height in this document is a minimum (`min-height`) except inside the virtualized lists (`data-table`, `log-viewer`), whose rows stay fixed. Form pages, banners, the transcript, the proposal card and the tool-call card reflow at any width; only data tables, the raw log view and code blocks claim two-dimensional scrolling. At 200% zoom on a 1,440px laptop the CSS viewport is 720px: yields 1–3 apply, and the panel's full-screen toggle is the accommodation for reading the transcript.

**Density.** This is an administrator's instrument, not a consumer app: 13px type, 36px rows, 32px controls, 6px radii. Whitespace is spent between regions, not inside tables.

Motion follows the same restraint: 120ms opacity or transform fades, the panel's width change to and from `{spacing.panel-home}` over 120ms, no bounce, no slide-in for the panel. The change highlight settles over 2s and then holds. Users who set `prefers-reduced-motion` get instant state changes, no width transition and no skeleton pulse.

The robot from the logo: [imports/robot-avatar.png](imports/robot-avatar.png), the 256px file, or [imports/robot-avatar-64.png](imports/robot-avatar-64.png), the 64px file, wherever it renders below 32px. #### `tool-call-card`

#### `proposal-card`

The card the agent's write tool produces — the one thing in the transcript that asks for a decision. `{colors.surface-container-lowest}`, 1px `{colors.outline-variant}`, a 4px `{colors.agent-accent}` bar on the left edge, `{rounded.md}`, elevation 1. Labels and captions are `{typography.caption}`; the agent's text is `{typography.body}` — running prose never drops below 13px. The card reflows at any panel width. Copy, lifecycle, the 10-minute window and where focus goes at each transition: EXPERIENCE.md › Component Patterns › proposal-card and › Agent Write Lifecycle. Anatomy:

1. **Header** (8px 12px, 1px `{colors.outline-variant}` bottom edge): `avatar-agent` at 20px, the title ("Proposal · <entity type> <name>") in `{typography.title}` `{colors.primary}` with the entity in `{typography.code}`, and at the right the countdown caption in `{typography.caption}` `{colors.on-surface-variant}`. At 1:00 remaining the caption turns `{colors.warning}` (7.06:1 light / 12.25:1 dark on the card) and stays so to 0:00.
2. **Diff**: the changed fields first, one `diff-row` each. The fields the payload also sends unchanged are collapsed beneath them under a disclosure row — 24px, `{typography.caption}` `{colors.on-surface-variant}`, a 16px chevron, reading "N unchanged fields" — closed by default; open, they list as unchanged `diff-row`s. The card's default height is two changed rows and one disclosure line, whatever the payload carries.
3. **Agent's text**: two blocks on `{colors.agent-container}` with `{colors.on-agent-container}` `{typography.body}` text, `{rounded.sm}`, 8px 10px padding, each under a `{typography.label}` uppercase heading in `{colors.agent-accent}` — "Agent's rationale" and "Expected impact" — with a small "agent" tag beside the heading (`{typography.label}` `{colors.on-agent-accent}` on `{colors.agent-accent}`, `{rounded.sm}`, 1px 5px, sentence case). This tint marks the model's words and appears nowhere else.
4. **Reverse** line where a reversal exists: the "Reverse:" lead in `{colors.on-surface}` 600, the description in `{colors.on-surface-variant}`.
5. **Confirmation inputs** when required: a `typed-name-field` for a destructive write; a `masked-secret-field` for each secret-typed field. A destructive proposal also gets a 3px `{colors.destructive}` bar *under* the header rule and its Confirm button becomes `button-destructive`.
6. **Footer** (1px `{colors.outline-variant}` top edge, 8px 12px): the caption "Runs as <user name>, with your privileges.", then Confirm as `button-primary`, Cancel as `button-text`, and beneath them, while the card is live, the **guard caption** in `{typography.caption}` `{colors.on-surface-variant}` (7.63:1) — the line that says to confirm here because sending a message cancels the proposal; EXPERIENCE.md owns its words and any second caption line. The footer strip is also the card's status line: it is the focus destination after Confirm, Cancel and expiry and shows `focus-ring` when it holds focus.

#### `diff-row`

A 24px row inside the proposal card: the field label in a 76px column, `{typography.caption}` `{colors.on-surface-variant}`; then the before value in `{typography.code}` `{colors.destructive}` with a line-through (6.54:1 on the card), an arrow `→` in `{colors.on-surface-variant}` with 6px either side, and the after value in `{typography.code}` at 600 `{colors.success}` (6.54:1 / 11.87:1). These are the diff colors in this document and in EXPERIENCE.md; the `on-surface-variant` / `on-surface` pairing is retired. Changed rows come first; unchanged fields (the rest of the payload) sit under the card's "N unchanged fields" disclosure and show the value once in `{typography.code}` `{colors.on-surface-variant}` with no arrow. Secret values render as `••••••••` on both sides; empty values read "(none)". Long values wrap to a second line rather than truncate; the row grows. Visually the strike-through and the weight carry the direction, not color alone; the spoken form ("was … now …", the arrow hidden from assistive tech) is EXPERIENCE.md's.

#### `banner`

An inline notice, never floating: `{rounded.md}`, 8px 10px padding, `{typography.caption}`, a 14px glyph (the interim Material Symbols glyph until the owner's icons) at the left with an 8px gap, text wrapping at any width, an optional `button-text`-shaped link at the end (the auditing-off banner uses it for administrators). A banner that receives focus (the form error summary, an alert) shows `focus-ring`. Variants: **info** `{colors.info}` on `{colors.info-container}` (turn lock, gate reminder, navigation notices); **warning** `{colors.warning}` on `{colors.warning-container}` (auditing off, target changed, secret-like message about to be sent); **error** `{colors.on-error-container}` on `{colors.error-container}` (turn ended with an error, form error summary, instance unreachable); **restrained** `{colors.restrained}` on `{colors.restrained-container}` (kill switch, read-only enforced); **egress** `{colors.egress-warning}` on `{colors.egress-warning-container}` (plain-HTTP acknowledgment). Banners in the panel span its width minus `{spacing.panel-gutter}`; in the content they span the form or table width. A dismissible banner has a 20px close icon button at the right; the auditing-off and kill-switch banners are not dismissible.

### Feedback and dialogs

What confirms or interrupts: the toast, the confirmation dialog, and the two fields a confirmation asks the user to fill.

#### `toast`

For a change on a screen that is not open: `{colors.inverse-surface}` with `{colors.inverse-on-surface}` `{typography.body}`, `{rounded.md}`, `{spacing.3}` padding, at the bottom-right of the **content area** — `{spacing.4}` above the status bar, and offset from the right edge by the panel's live width so no toast ever overlays the panel — stacking upward to three. Content: the change in one sentence, then an "Open in <screen>" link that swaps with the mode because the inverse surface flips — `{colors.secondary-dark}` in light (7.42:1), `{colors.secondary}` in dark (4.64:1 — marginal, guard with a test) — plus a 20px close icon. Timing, the focus-pause rule and what happens beyond three: EXPERIENCE.md › Component Patterns › toast. Toasts are never used for errors (those are banners) and never for confirmations of what the user just did on the open screen (the row highlight is the confirmation). [AMENDED 2026-09-21, Epic 5 merge gate (DW-1412): bottom-right of the *viewport* put the toast over the docked panel and covered the panel's own Send button at 1440×900 — `elementFromPoint` on the button's center returned the toast, not the button. A toast that covers the primary control of the surface it reports on defeats itself. The offset tracks the panel's live width rather than a fixed token because the panel is resizable.]

#### `confirm-dialog`

A Material dialog for the set of dialogs listed in EXPERIENCE.md › Information Architecture › Dialogs (set password, resource grant, resource editor, terminate process, remove locks, audit event detail, every delete confirmation, and the three warnings that precede a non-delete write): 440px, `{colors.surface-container-lowest}`, `{rounded.lg}`, elevation 3, `{spacing.6}` padding, Material's scrim. Title in `{typography.title}` naming the action and the target ("Delete web application /csp/myapp?" with the target in `{typography.code}`); body in `{typography.body}` stating the consequence ("no scheduled task will run until it is resumed"); any inputs (`typed-name-field`, the error-to-job checkbox, the password fields); actions right-aligned: Cancel as `button-text`, the action as `button-primary`, or as `button-destructive` when it deletes, terminates or removes. Focus lands on the first input, or on Cancel where the dialog has none. No dialog stacks on another.

#### `typed-name-field`

The destructive confirmation: a Material outlined field at `{spacing.input-height}` whose label reads "Type /csp/myapp to confirm" with the target in `{typography.code}`, input text in `{typography.code}`, 1px `{colors.outline}` border, `{rounded.md}`; focus 2px `{colors.secondary}` plus `focus-ring`; helper text "Must match exactly" in `{colors.on-surface-variant}`. The confirming button stays unavailable until the value matches exactly; a mismatch on blur shows a 2px `{colors.destructive}` border and the mismatch message in `{colors.destructive}` — reported, never silent, per the accessibility review (a disabled Delete with no reason gives a screen-reader user nothing). Used identically in the proposal card and in delete dialogs, so the two paths look the same.

#### `masked-secret-field`

For secret-typed values entered at confirmation or in agent configuration: a Material outlined password field at `{spacing.input-height}`, `{typography.code}` dots, 1px `{colors.outline}`, `{rounded.md}`, a 20px reveal toggle in `{colors.on-surface-variant}` at the right that shows the value only while pressed or toggled, helper text in `{colors.on-surface-variant}` (its words per field: EXPERIENCE.md). Focus 2px `{colors.secondary}` plus `focus-ring`. The field never pre-fills, never echoes a stored value, and pastes are accepted without trimming. Provider key-shape errors show as a `{colors.destructive}` border with the shape hint beneath.

### Buttons

All four are Material 3 buttons at `{spacing.control-height}` (32px), `{rounded.full}`, `{typography.body}` at 600, 0 `{spacing.4}` padding (text buttons 0 10px), a 6px gap before a leading icon, state layers at Material's 8% hover / 12% pressed, 38% opacity when unavailable, `focus-ring` on focus (the inner halo is 5.76:1 against the teal fill in light mode, 10.31:1 in dark; 6.54:1 / 10.54:1 against the destructive fill). Privilege-gated buttons are not dimmed: they take `{colors.restrained}` text on a `{colors.restrained-container}` fill (5.42:1 / 6.73:1), no hover, and the resource tooltip on hover and focus — buttons take DOM focus, so the tooltip reaches keyboard users.

#### `button-primary`

Filled: `{colors.secondary}` with `{colors.on-secondary}` (5.76:1 / 7.95:1) — teal, never `{colors.primary}`. One per bar, card footer or dialog — the thing you came to do (Create, Save, Confirm, Test connection, Sign in, and Send while nothing is running and no proposal is live).

#### `button-secondary`

Outlined: transparent with a 1px `{colors.outline}` border and `{colors.secondary}` text; state layer `{colors.secondary}`. View menus, Re-propose, Load newer, Raw, the classic-link card's action, Send while a proposal is live, Stop while a turn runs, Cancel where Cancel must be prominent.

#### `button-text`

`{colors.secondary}` text on nothing; state layer `{colors.secondary}`. Cancel beside a primary, secondary command-bar actions, links inside banners, "Open" in Home's suggested view.

#### `button-destructive`

Filled `{colors.destructive}` with `{colors.on-destructive}`; state layer `{colors.on-destructive}`. Only inside a `confirm-dialog` or a destructive `proposal-card`, only after the typed name matches, and only ever labeled with the verb (Delete, Terminate, Remove) — never "OK" or "Yes". It never appears in a command bar or a row menu; those use `{colors.destructive}` text and open the dialog.

### Focus

The one indicator every interactive element in the product shares, on each of the three grounds it can land on.

#### `focus-ring`

A two-tone ring: 2px `{colors.focus-ring}` outside a 2px `{colors.focus-ring-inner}` halo, offset 0 so the halo touches the control (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px inner, 0 0 0 4px outer`), following the control's radius. It has three grounds. On the surfaces and the sheet, the two-tone ring above. On the chrome (rail, header, status bar), 2px `{colors.focus-ring-dark}` outside a 2px `{colors.shell}` halo in both modes; the light-mode `{colors.focus-ring}` is never drawn there (rule 1). On the code surface, 2px `{colors.focus-ring-dark}` outside 2px `{colors.code-surface}`. Every pair clears its floor, including the halo against each filled control it can touch — the measured ratios are in §Colors › Contrast. Inside a table row or side-bar entry the ring is drawn inset so it is not clipped. It shows for keyboard and programmatic focus (`:focus-visible`), not for mouse clicks, and it is never replaced by a color change alone. A "Skip to content" link is the first Tab stop: hidden until focused, then drawn at the top-left over the header with the `button-primary` treatment and the on-chrome ring.

## Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Write "agent co-pilot" for the feature, "the agent" for the software and "the panel" for the UI. | Write "co-pilot" alone, anywhere — UI copy, tooltips, code comments, commit messages. |
| Vendor every font, icon and script in the bundle; the shell loads with no reachable CDN. | Load a font, icon set or script from a CDN, a Google Fonts URL or any host other than the instance. |
| Use `{colors.secondary}` (teal) for every button, link, selection and active indicator, and reserve yellow for the agent's avatar ground, its rationale and expected impact, the proposal bar, and the changed row or field. | Put yellow — `agent-accent`, `agent-container`, `change-highlight` or the robot yellow — on a button, link, switch, chip or badge. |
| | Use yellow as a warning color; warnings are `{colors.warning}` on `{colors.warning-container}`. |
| Show a privilege-gated entry disabled and readable, with a tooltip naming the required resource. | Hide a screen, action, tile or menu item the user lacks privilege for. |
| Show a proposal card restored from a reloaded transcript as expired, with Re-propose. | Show a restored card as live, with Confirm still available. |
| Keep the composer editable and Send present (as Stop) while a turn runs; keep every control that holds focus in the DOM until focus has a named destination. | Natively disable the composer, or remove Confirm from under the keyboard. |
| Confine the gradient to the header and to the two 2px hairlines (panel header rule, panel edge stripe), keeping the logo's vivid teal `#2090A0` to their far stop; draw the active rail indicator solid `{colors.secondary-dark}`. | Put `#2090A0` under text, fill any area with it, or bring back the gradient rail indicator (1.94:1 at its top). |
| | Fill cards, buttons, the status bar or the panel with the gradient, or draw a hairline gradient under a non-text floor it cannot meet. |
| Use the reversed horizontal lockup (`imports/OcuPilot-Lockup-horizontal-reversed.png`) at 32px directly on the shell, the mark alone where only the mark fits, the light lockup on light grounds, and the robot crop as the agent's avatar. | Put the lockup on a white plate, use the stacked PNG in the header, typeset the wordmark in Inter or any face, use a wordmark-only lockup, put the navy-wordmark file on the chrome, or use a robot glyph that is not the crop. |
| Use `{colors.restrained}` at full opacity for what the agent or the portal is holding back — blocked, expired, canceled, stopped, kill-switched, gated — keeping the agent's text on its tint. | Fade anything the user must read with opacity — 60% over the sheet fails AA. |
| | Use `error` or `destructive` for read-only blocks and expiry — nothing went wrong. |
| Draw the attention dot in `{colors.agent-accent-dark}` in both modes. | Draw it in light-mode `agent-accent` (1.80:1 on the shell). |
| Keep the side bar at 240px with no resize affordance; the panel is the only resizable edge. | Add a sash, grip or drag handle to the side bar. |
| Draw the focus ring on the chrome with `{colors.focus-ring-dark}` and a `{colors.shell}` halo in both modes. | Draw the light-mode `{colors.focus-ring}` on the navy chrome (1.18:1). |
| Put the agent's own words on `{colors.agent-container}` under the "Agent's rationale" and "Expected impact" headings. | Tint any other text with `agent-container`, or let the model's words appear without the tint and heading. |
| Give a destructive action a `{colors.destructive}` text item that opens a dialog, and the filled `button-destructive` only inside that dialog or card after the typed name matches. | Put a filled red button in a command bar or a row menu, or label a destructive button "OK". |
| Set identifiers (paths, classes, resources, pids, namespaces) in `{typography.code}` everywhere. | Set an identifier in the sans, or a sentence in the monospace. |
| Keep 13px body, 36px rows, 32px controls and 6px radii; spend whitespace between regions. | Loosen the density to "breathe" (this is an instrument, not a landing page), or tighten below 11px. |
| Keep the chrome navy in both modes and layer the content with Lantern's containers. | Invert the chrome in dark mode, or use elevation shadows for hierarchy inside a screen. |
| Pair every status color with its container (`success` on `success-container`, and so on) and always add the word. | Signal a state by color alone, or mix a role with another role's container. |
| Give every interactive element a visible focus state, inset where it would clip. | Remove outlines, rely on a color change for focus, or let the focus ring clip inside a scrolling table. |
| Show the server-flag badge in the status bar with the flag's own color pair, Live in red. | Move the badge into the header, or style all four flags alike. |
| Keep the panel present on every route with its resize handle and full-screen toggle. | Add a close control to the panel in Release 1, or turn it into an overlay or bottom sheet on narrow viewports. |
