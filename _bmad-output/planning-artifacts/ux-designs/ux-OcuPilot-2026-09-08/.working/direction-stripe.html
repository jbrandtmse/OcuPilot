Direction B — Stripe

White chrome, white paper, and one unmistakable brand stripe: the logo's navy-to-teal-to-mint gradient runs under the header and reappears only where something is active (rail entry, side-bar item, the top edge of a proposal card). The brand is carried by the content itself — navy display type, teal links, robot yellow on the agent and on the changed row — rather than by painting the frame.

More air than Direction A: 14px body, 48px rows, 12px radii, soft shadows, pill-shaped fields, so the portal reads as calm and modern rather than as a console. Dark mode inverts the surfaces and keeps the stripe and gradient markers untouched, so the brand cue survives both themes.
