Direction C — Nightwatch

Dark-first: the whole UI sits on the logo's deepest navy with the chrome one layer darker, the way VS Code Dark stacks activity bar, side bar and editor. The brand is not painted onto the frame — it signals: teal is every action and active state, robot yellow is the agent and the thing that just changed, and the only gradient is the hairline under the panel title. Identifiers (paths, classes, namespaces, timestamps) are monospace, which gives the screens the ops-console voice an administrator expects at 2 a.m.

Density is medium-dense (13px, 38px rows, 6px radii) with M3 pill buttons in filled teal. The light flip keeps the dark chrome and lightens only the content and panel, which makes light-mode Nightwatch converge on Direction A — one shell, two moods.
